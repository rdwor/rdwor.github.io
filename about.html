01

A little about me

I’m an Aerospace Engineering student at Georgia Tech with a minor in Computing and Intelligence. I’ve always enjoyed figuring out how things work, asking questions, and finding better ways to solve problems.

But the part of engineering I enjoy most has rarely been just the technical problem. I like working with people, learning how different groups think, and figuring out how to turn an idea or analysis into something useful in the real world.

That same curiosity carries into the rest of my life. Music, travel, sports, art, and conversation all give me different ways to learn, create, and connect with people.

02

Engineering

My experiences at Delta Air Lines and Voith helped me realize that I’m especially drawn to engineering problems that exist in the real world — problems involving reliability, operations, people, and systems that have to keep working every day.

At Delta, I worked in A320 Family fleet engineering and saw how technical decisions connect to maintenance, airport operations, suppliers, safety, and reliability across a large aircraft fleet.

At Voith, I got a different perspective through manufacturing and operations, working on problems involving process improvement, tracking, and the day-to-day needs of a facility.

Those experiences made me interested not only in engineering itself, but in how technical teams, operations, and organizations work together to solve difficult problems.

03

Music

Music has been a major part of my life for years. I play trombone with the Atlanta Concert Band and the Georgia Tech Marching Band.

I love that music is both individual and collaborative. You have to know your own part, but the experience only really works when everyone listens to one another and contributes to something larger.

Performing has also given me some of my favorite memories and introduced me to people I probably never would have met otherwise.

04

Travel + the arts

I love traveling because it pushes me outside of what is familiar. Experiencing different cities, cultures, food, music, architecture, and art changes the way I look at the world.

Studying abroad through Georgia Tech-Europe reinforced that for me. I enjoy seeing how people live differently, what they value, and how different places develop their own identity.

Art, music, film, and design are also things I naturally gravitate toward. I like the way creative work can communicate an idea or feeling in ways that technical language sometimes cannot.

05

Sports

I’ve always enjoyed sports, especially running and basketball. I like the contrast between them: basketball is collaborative, competitive, and fast-moving, while running gives me time to think, reset, and challenge myself individually.

Both give me an outlet outside of school and engineering and have always been an important part of how I spend my time.

06

Community

Staying involved in the communities around me is important to me. I’ve taken on student leadership roles at Georgia Tech, volunteered with Atlanta Mission, and stayed involved with organizations outside of engineering.

I especially enjoy opportunities where I can meet people with different experiences, help someone move forward, or contribute to a group working toward something together.

What connects it all

I like finding different ways to connect with people and understand the world around me.

Engineering gives me a way to solve problems with other people. Music lets me create with them. Sports create shared challenges. Travel exposes me to perspectives I would not encounter otherwise. They may look like very different interests, but for me they all come back to curiosity, collaboration, and connection.
